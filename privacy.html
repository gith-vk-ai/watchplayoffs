Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information WatchPlayoffs.com ("WatchPlayoffs," "we," "us," or "our") collects from visitors, how it is used, and the choices available to you. By using WatchPlayoffs.com (the "Site"), you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Automatically Collect

Like most websites, WatchPlayoffs.com and its hosting infrastructure automatically receive certain information when you visit, including your IP address, general device and browser information, the pages you request, referring pages, and the date and time of your visit. This information is generated by standard web server and content-delivery operations and is used for purposes such as serving pages correctly, maintaining site performance, and protecting against abuse.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

WatchPlayoffs.com does not currently set its own tracking or advertising cookies. If you follow a link to a third-party ticket marketplace or other outbound link, that third-party site may set its own cookies or similar technologies subject to its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review.

Analytics

WatchPlayoffs.com does not currently use a third-party analytics service. If we add analytics, advertising, or similar measurement tools in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to name the provider and describe how it is used before that change takes effect.

Affiliate Links & Tracking

Some links on WatchPlayoffs.com, particularly ticket-related links, are affiliate links. These links may include a tracking parameter or identifier that allows our affiliate partner to recognize that a visit or purchase originated from WatchPlayoffs.com, so that we may earn a commission as described in our Affiliate & Ticket Marketplace Disclosure. This tracking is handled by the third-party partner once you leave our Site.

Outbound Links to Third-Party Ticket Marketplaces

WatchPlayoffs.com links to third-party ticket marketplaces and service providers. Once you leave WatchPlayoffs.com, any information you provide — including payment information, contact details, or account information — is collected and processed by that third party under its own privacy policy, not this one. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party site before providing personal information.

Third-Party Services

WatchPlayoffs.com is a static website hosted and delivered through a third-party hosting and content-delivery provider. That provider may process standard technical and network data (such as IP addresses and request logs) as part of operating its infrastructure and keeping the Site secure and available.

Payments

WatchPlayoffs.com itself does not process ticket purchases or payment card information. All ticket transactions are completed on the third-party ticket marketplace's own website or platform.

Data Retention

We retain the limited technical information described above only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes outlined in this policy, such as site operation, security, and troubleshooting.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding personal information collected about you, such as the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information. Because WatchPlayoffs.com collects very limited personal information directly, most requests relating to a ticket purchase should be directed to the third-party ticket marketplace where the transaction took place. For questions about this policy, you can contact us using the details below.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to a static informational website to help protect the limited information associated with your visit. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

WatchPlayoffs.com is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

International Visitors

WatchPlayoffs.com is operated with a focus on visitors in the United States. If you access the Site from outside the United States, your information may be processed in the United States or other locations where our hosting infrastructure operates, which may have different data protection laws than your country of residence.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Site or applicable law. Changes take effect once posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@watchplayoffs.com.
